About Us

Company & brand identity

AGRITECH HUB is a business app built for electricians, plumbers and small contractors in Tanzania.

Our goal is to end handwritten quotations. You pick materials from your inventory, the app calculates the cost, and you send a PDF quotation or invoice to your customer in minutes.

What we stand for

Simple, lightweight tools that work offline, because site work doesn't wait for a signal. Your data stays on your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Downloading is free. Pro features use a license (for example quarterly). Contact us for license details.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All data is stored on your phone, so you can create quotations and invoices without internet.

Is my data safe?

Your data is kept on your own phone. We do not sell or share your customers' information.

Which phones are supported?

Any Android phone running version 5.0 or newer.

Can I send invoices to customers?

Yes. Save as PDF, print, or share directly through WhatsApp, email and more.
